SND sound files: terms of use (a summary)
https://snd.dev/

The SND sound files are made by Dentsu's SND project. The full terms are on
snd.dev; this is what they allow and ask, as read when the files were added.

- The sounds are free to use in personal and commercial work, including
  inside apps and services, with no permission needed.
- Selling or sharing the sound files on their own, as they are, is not
  allowed. They may only be passed on as part of a work that uses them.
- A sound may not be used as a trademark or a sound logo.
- Copyright stays with the sound designer and Dentsu.
- Credit is not required, and is welcome.
